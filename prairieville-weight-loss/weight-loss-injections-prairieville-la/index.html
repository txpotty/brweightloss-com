    How Weight Loss Injections Work in Prairieville, LA

    How Weight Loss Injections Work in Prairieville

    Most modern weight loss shots are GLP-1 medicines. These drugs mimic a gut hormone released after meals, keeping food in the stomach longer, telling the brain you are full and steadying blood sugar. Semaglutide acts on GLP-1 alone. Tirzepatide acts on both GLP-1 and GIP receptors, which may explain its larger average effect in trials.

    Who Weight Loss Injections Are For

    FDA-approved injections are for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher. Adults with a BMI of 27 or more also qualify if they have a weight-related condition such as high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol or sleep apnea. Diet and activity still count. Pregnancy and some thyroid cancer histories rule them out.

    Injection Dosing Schedule in Prairieville

    Every weekly injection starts low. Wegovy starts at 0.25 mg and moves up every four weeks to 0.5, 1, 1.7 and finally 2.4 mg, with some patients holding at 1.7 mg. Zepbound begins at 2.5 mg and can go up by 2.5 mg at a time to a top of 15 mg. Same day each week.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Appetite often drops in the first week. The first month tends to show small changes on the scale since the dose is low, while stomach upset clusters right after each shot. Large trials reported average losses near 15 percent with semaglutide 2.4 mg over 68 weeks and near 20 percent with tirzepatide 15 mg over 72 weeks. Results vary.

    Injection Side Effects in Prairieville

    Nausea is the classic one. Diarrhea, constipation, vomiting, heartburn and mild redness at the injection site are also common, especially in the days after starting or raising the dose. Smaller meals, protein first and plenty of water help most people. Rarer serious risks include pancreatitis, gallbladder problems and dehydration-related kidney strain.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening starts with BMI and medical history. Next comes your medication list, with attention to insulin, glipizide-type drugs and oral contraceptives, which tirzepatide can make less effective around dose increases. A family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 is a hard stop. Pregnancy and breastfeeding are too.

    Brand-Name vs Compounded Injections in Prairieville

    Wegovy and Zepbound are FDA-approved for weight management. Ozempic and Mounjaro share those ingredients but carry type 2 diabetes labels instead. Compounded versions of semaglutide and tirzepatide come from licensed compounding pharmacies and lack FDA approval, which means no FDA review of their safety, effectiveness or manufacturing. Availability drives some people toward them.
